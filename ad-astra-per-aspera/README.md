# Ad Astra Per Aspera

Rockets, space stations and four planets for Minecraft **26.2** (Fabric). A port of
[Ad Astra](https://github.com/terrarium-earth/Ad-Astra) by Terrarium, rebuilt for Fabric only.

- **Fly to space.** Build rockets at the NASA Workbench, fuel them from the Fuel Refinery and launch
  to the Moon, Mars, Venus, Mercury and Glacio, or to the orbit of each.
- **Survive there.** No air, low gravity, extreme heat and cold. Space suits, oxygen distributors,
  gravity normalizers and the oxygen loader keep you alive.
- **Planet skies.** Every planet has its own sun, moons and coloured stars; Venus has acid rain
  and its own clouds.
- **Machines and energy.** Compressor, Etrionic Blast Furnace, Cryo Freezer, solar panels, cables
  and pipes. Energy uses Team Reborn Energy and fluids use the Fabric Transfer API, so it connects
  to other tech mods.

The mod id stays `ad_astra`, so worlds and resource packs made for Ad Astra keep working.

Install it on both client and server. Needs Fabric API; ResourcefulLib and ResourcefulConfig are bundled inside.

## Works with Starlight

On the Overworld, [Starlight](../starlight) draws the night sky as usual. In the Ad Astra
dimensions this mod draws the planet's own sky, and Starlight's stars are not shown there, except
in Earth orbit: there Starlight's real constellations fill the sky behind the Earth and the Sun.
